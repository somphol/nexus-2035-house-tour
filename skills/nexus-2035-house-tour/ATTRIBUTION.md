## Assets

Every mesh, material, texture, sky, city block, vehicle, plant and light is generated at
runtime. The project ships no 3D models, image textures or audio files. The repository's
PNG screenshots and social card are documentation, not runtime assets.

## Data

The tour state is local to the browser. No analytics, telemetry service or network request
is made by the application beyond the CDN scripts and fonts.
